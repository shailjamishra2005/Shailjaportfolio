LegacyVault

A platform for the messages you are not ready to send yet. Built for the people who matter most.

Brand Positioning GTM Strategy Digital Marketing Product Launch

The Idea

Some things are too important to say too soon.

LegacyVault is a platform that lets you write messages, letters and recordings today and schedule them to be delivered to your loved ones at a future date — a birthday ten years from now, a wedding day, a graduation, or whenever you choose.

The insight behind it is simple: the things we most want to say to the people we love are often the things we keep putting off. LegacyVault removes the excuse of timing and makes the intention permanent.

The User Journey

How it works

01

Write or record your message

Text, audio or video. As long or as short as you need. The platform saves it securely and privately.

02

Set the delivery conditions

A specific date, a life event trigger or a conditional delivery based on circumstances you define. You stay in control.

03

Name your recipient

Add the person's details. They do not need to know the message exists until the moment it arrives.

04

The message arrives

On the day you chose, your words find the person you wrote them for. Exactly as you intended. Exactly on time.

Go-to-Market Strategy

Reaching people at emotional moments

The GTM strategy was built around emotional triggers rather than demographic segments. The people most likely to use LegacyVault are not defined by age or income. They are defined by moments — a new parent, someone facing illness, a person who simply thinks ahead about the people they love.

✦

Emotional content marketing

Stories of real messages delivered at real moments. User testimonials as the primary acquisition channel.

✦

Partnership with grief and life-event platforms

Integrations with wedding planners, estate services and parenting communities where intent is highest.

✦

Freemium with depth

Free tier for one message. Paid tiers unlock storage, multiple recipients and advanced delivery conditions.

"

The best marketing does not sell a product. It reminds people of something they already wanted to do.

Key Learning

LegacyVault taught me that the most powerful brand positioning is not about features. It is about the feeling a product creates in the moment someone realises they need it. Building the GTM around emotional triggers rather than demographics was the most important strategic decision in the whole pitch.
